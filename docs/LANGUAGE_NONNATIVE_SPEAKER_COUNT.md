# Non-native (L2) speakers per language

Figures are in millions of second-language (L2) speakers, highest first. They were collected on 2026-11-06.

Sources:
- **E**: Ethnologue 2026, as given in Wikipedia's
  [List of languages by total number of speakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_languages_by_total_number_of_speakers).
  That list only covers languages with 50M+ total speakers.
- **W**: the infobox of the language's own Wikipedia article. Years vary, so these figures are
  less comparable than the E ones.
